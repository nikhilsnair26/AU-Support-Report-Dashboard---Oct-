40 Tickets Still Open

87.9% of October tickets are closed. The 40 still open are 28 Pending, 8 On Hold and 4 Open; 12 are older than 14 days as of 2 Nov 2026, led by #5153 Tooth savers (On Hold, 31 days). Advaith holds 26 of the 40.

Real Issues Have the Biggest Backlog

15 of 66 Real Issue / Incident tickets are still open (22.7%), the highest backlog of any main bucket. Real faults outnumber how-to requests this month — 66 vs. 40.

Read Resolution Times With Care

The October export has created dates but no times, so resolution is measured from the start of the created day. That adds roughly 8 hrs to every ticket, so absolute hours read high — compare agents, buckets and categories with each other rather than against fixed targets.

Opportunity — Holiday Calendar Self-Serve

Holiday Calendar is the biggest single driver: 80 tickets (24.2% of the month), all closed. Configuration / Admin is 56.4% of volume and 49.8% of resolution time — repeatable work suited to self-serve.

Business Critical Long Tail

26 Business Critical / Phone Impacting tickets (7.9%), 4 still open. P90 is 9.9 days. Devices is the main driver — 15 of the 26, with the Devices feature showing a P90 of 9.3 days.

Enquiries Run Longest

Only 7 tickets ran past 12.5 days, but they hold 17.7% of all resolution hours. The two longest are Enquiry tickets — #5219 First Choice Dental (22.3 days) and #5211 Norlane Dental (21.6 days). Enquiry overall has a P90 of 18.6 days.

How to Read This Dashboard
Resolution timeElapsed time from ticket created to solved, including nights, weekends and time waiting on the customer — not agent effort hours. This month it is measured from the start of the created day. Shown in hours below 48 hrs and in days from 48 hrs.
MedianThe typical ticket: half resolved faster, half slower. Not affected by a few very long tickets.
P9090% of tickets were resolved within this time. Shows how long the slowest tickets take.
Backlog %Share of tickets still Open, Pending or On Hold at the time of the data.
Time shareShare of all resolution hours that a bucket, agent or category accounts for.
Configuration / Admin ServiceSetup and change requests — holiday calendars, users, agents, call flows.
Real Issue / IncidentActual faults or errors that need fixing.
Business Critical / Phone ImpactingIssues stopping or degrading the practice's phones — the severity measure in place of priority. Assigned from Category / Feature and phone-impacting words in the ticket subject (e.g. not ringing, no service, offline).
How-to / Clarification / TrainingGuidance and questions rather than a fault to fix.
Product / Commercial / InternalIntegrations, feature requests, billing and internal tickets.
Unclassified / Data GapNo Category set.
Agent statusAction Needed: median over 24 hrs or backlog over 20%. Monitor: median over 8 hrs, P90 over 7 days, or backlog over 10%. Otherwise On Track.
Organisation riskHigh: backlog over 30%. Med: backlog over 8% or P90 over 7 days. Otherwise Low.
Note: Resolution hours are elapsed time from the start of the ticket's created date to its solved timestamp — not agent effort hours.
